We describe a bootstrap stability screening procedure for scarce tabular
regression data. The analyst first constructs a complete-case design matrix for
the proposed numeric outcome and candidate numeric predictors. Fully missing
rows are removed, numeric-like fields are coerced, and predictors with zero
variance are excluded. The method then draws repeated bootstrap samples with
replacement from the complete-case data. In each bootstrap sample, an
interpretable regression model is fit and the coefficient signs are recorded.
Variables whose coefficient signs are stable across bootstrap samples are
retained. The final model is fit on the complete-case data and evaluated using
held-out or cross-validated fit. The method reports the number of complete
cases, the number of bootstrap samples, coefficient sign stability, final fit
metrics, diagnostics, robustness summaries, and warnings. The assumptions are
that enough complete cases exist for resampling and that the outcome and
candidate predictors are not constant.
